ExPRSweb

The archived November 2021 release reports evaluations of polygenic risk scores for common health-related exposures.

Portal reporting instructions

ExPRSweb is for research only. It is not intended to diagnose or interpret individual results or provide medical advice.

This release includes more than 336 filtered scores across more than 28 retained traits, evaluated in the Michigan Genomics Initiative and UK Biobank.

In this release

The portal reports performance, calibration, discrimination, and phenome-wide results. It also provides methods and downloads for scores related to body mass index, smoking, blood pressure, lipids, and other exposures.

Release date

This is the November 20, 2021 release. It remains unchanged so the portal and downloads continue to match the published analysis.

Citation

When using this release, cite Ma and colleagues, American Journal of Human Genetics (2022), doi:10.1016/j.ajhg.2022.09.001.
