PRSweb Research Portals

Compare published polygenic risk scores evaluated in the Michigan Genomics Initiative (MGI) and UK Biobank, and explore phenome-wide associations and downloadable files.

PRSweb is for research only. It is not intended to diagnose or interpret individual results or provide medical advice.

What PRSweb includes

  • Score definitions, comparisons, and performance results.
  • Phenome-wide association results linked to individual scores.
  • Score files, phenotype mappings, release manifests, and related downloads.
  • A peer-reviewed publication for each release.

Three archived releases

  • Cancer PRSweb covers more than 305 filtered scores across more than 39 retained traits in its June 2020 release.
  • ExPRSweb covers more than 336 filtered scores across more than 28 retained exposure traits in its November 2021 release.
  • Skin Cancer PRSweb is the original June 2019 catalog of 20 scores across four traits.

The archived releases remain unchanged, so their pages and downloads continue to match the corresponding papers.

Data behind the portal

PRSweb publishes score files and result tables, not participant-level data from the Michigan Genomics Initiative or UK Biobank.

When reporting results, cite the paper associated with the specific release you used.

No collection-wide reuse license is stated for the portal artifacts. Terms attached to the source score models may also apply; contact the maintainer before redistributing portal files.
