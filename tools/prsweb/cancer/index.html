Cancer PRSweb

The archived June 2020 release reports evaluations of cancer polygenic risk scores in the Michigan Genomics Initiative (MGI) and UK Biobank.

Portal reporting instructions

Cancer PRSweb is for research only. It is not intended to diagnose or interpret individual results or provide medical advice.

This release includes more than 305 filtered scores across more than 39 retained traits.

In this release

The portal includes score definitions, performance results, phenome-wide associations, phenotype mappings, and downloads. Use it to compare candidate cancer scores or reproduce the evaluation reported in the 2020 paper.

Release date

This is the June 8, 2020 release. It remains unchanged so the portal and downloads continue to match the published analysis.

Citation

When using this release, cite Fritsche and colleagues, American Journal of Human Genetics (2020), doi:10.1016/j.ajhg.2020.08.025.
