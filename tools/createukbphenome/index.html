createUKBphenome

A reproducible workflow for mapping authorized UK Biobank ICD-9 and ICD-10 data to PheWAS phenotypes.

The repository publishes research code, not UK Biobank participant data or a clinical tool.

The workflow produces PheWAS phenotype definitions and case-control matrices and uses the project’s current list of withdrawn participants.

When to use it

  • You are an approved UK Biobank researcher.
  • You need reproducible PheWAS phenotype definitions from diagnosis data.
  • You want to prepare case-control matrices for an authorized analysis.

UK Biobank access is required

The workflow expects locally downloaded UK Biobank data, genotype data, and the current withdrawal file. It must run under an approved UK Biobank project and follow that project’s access, security, withdrawal, and disclosure requirements.

UK Biobank now primarily provides research access through its Research Analysis Platform. Before relying on the repository’s local-file workflow, confirm that it remains appropriate for your authorized environment and current UK Biobank procedures.

Maintenance status

createUKBphenome is licensed under GPL-3.0. The repository has recent updates, but it does not name a current maintainer or provide a release, citation, or support policy.
