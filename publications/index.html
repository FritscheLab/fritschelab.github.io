Polygenic Risk Scores for Prediction of Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Thyroid Toxicity in Diverse Populations

Fritsche, L. G., Higgins, L. M., Schipper, M., Strohbehn, G., McMahon, B. H., Crivelli, S., Dai, X., Yoo, S., Vanderwerff, B., Bertucci-Richter, E., Elliott, D., Green, M. D., Bryant, A. K., Veterans Affairs Million Veteran Program

Clinical Cancer Research

Why it matters: Treatment-related outcomes include adverse effects as well as benefits. In 4,289 veterans treated with checkpoint inhibitors, an updated hypothyroidism polygenic score predicted thyroiditis in both the non-Hispanic White and Black patient groups, but not in chemotherapy controls. The older score, which was derived from European-ancestry data, did not predict thyroiditis in the Black patient group. This shows why scores need to be validated in each population and treatment setting.
