Genome-wide association testing with trait distributions

A workshop poster uses repeated glucose readings to illustrate genome-wide association testing with the full distribution of a trait.

Tai Yang presented Genome-Wide Association Testing for Distributional Phenotypes via Wasserstein–Fréchet Regression at the Institute for Mathematical and Statistical Innovation workshop Statistical Foundations for the Analysis of Non-Euclidean Data. The poster was coauthored with Junyoung Park, Lars G. Fritsche, and Irina Gaynanova. Workshop agenda

The poster applies the method to continuous glucose monitoring data from the Human Phenotype Project. The method tests genetic associations with the mean and with the shape of the distribution separately, then combines the two tests. View the poster
