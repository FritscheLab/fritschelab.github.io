Common and rare genetic variants in age-related macular degeneration

An international study of about 43,000 people identified common and rare genetic variants associated with age-related macular degeneration.

The International AMD Genomics Consortium brought together researchers from 26 institutions to study genetic susceptibility to age-related macular degeneration in about 43,000 people. The Nature Genetics study identified common and rare variants associated with the disease. Combining samples across institutions, including the U-M Kellogg Eye Center, helped identify rare variants that are difficult to detect in smaller studies.
