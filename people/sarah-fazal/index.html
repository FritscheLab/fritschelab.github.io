Research Fellow

Sarah Fazal, PhD

Sarah brings experience in human genetics and bioinformatics to the lab’s pain genetics and phenotyping research.

Research

Sarah is a human geneticist and bioinformatician. In her prior work, she developed computational approaches to detect and prioritize pathogenic repeat expansions in rare disease. These include RExPRT, a method that combines multiple signals to prioritize potentially pathogenic repeat expansions.

Current training

Sarah is a trainee in the University of Michigan HEAL PAIN T90/R90 Cohort Program. Her primary mentor is Lars Fritsche, with co-mentors Daniel Clauw, Andrew Schrepf, and Stephan Frangakis. She brings her experience in genomics and computational method development to the lab’s pain genetics and phenotyping research.
